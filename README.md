# 👤 About Me

I am a software engineer and data scientist specializing in building production-grade AI systems, scalable machine learning models, and data-driven solutions. With an analytical and product engineering mindset, I focus heavily on the complete data lifecycle—from robust automated data ingestion and preprocessing pipelines to deploying predictive modeling solutions and exploring complex generative AI workflows. My mission is to architect intelligent, secure, and performant AI architectures that bridge foundational data science with enterprise engineering.

* **Software Engineering & Data Science Core:** Strong engineering foundations in Python execution models, advanced relational schema design, data structures, and lifecycle optimization pipelines.
* **AI/ML & Deep Learning Expertise:** Designing predictive pipelines utilizing classical statistical modeling, ensemble modeling frameworks, sequence structures, and computer vision features.
* **Generative AI & Modern Workflows:** Actively researching context-aware Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) applications, large language models (LLMs), and framework agents.
* **Product Engineering Mindset:** Packaging model inference systems behind clean web interfaces, ensuring production reliability, reproducible experiments, and scalable performance metrics.
